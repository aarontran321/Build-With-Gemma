Optional third sensor. GPS only — iOS does not expose the barometric altimeter to web pages — so expect ±10–30 m and no fix at all indoors. It never drives the simulated descent; it gives the arbiter a time-to-ground budget when deciding whether a manoeuvre is affordable.

Point the camera at a textured scene (desk, poster, keyboard) in decent light. The camera signal is computed from pixels only — it never reads the IMU — so texture and light are what give it confidence.
